Behind the music Method, privacy & inspiration

What counts as a listen?

Music plays lasting at least 30 seconds. Listening time is the reported duration of those plays. Short skips, private-session plays, podcasts, audiobooks, and video exports are excluded. Years at the edges of the export can be partial. Track versions with different Spotify IDs count separately.

Chart thickness represents monthly listening time or plays. The ten leading artists in the selected period have their own colors; all others are grouped. Smooth curves connect monthly observations; use tooltips for measured values.

Just the music.

Only Devin’s first name, public music metadata, and aggregate listening statistics are included. No account details, IP addresses, countries, device information, exact listening timestamps, or original exports. There are no playlist records in this export. No analytics, cookies, or Spotify login.

The listening clock and calendar use UTC, the export’s time basis. They do not reveal a location or infer a home time zone.

Inspired by the good stuff.

Lee Byron’s streamgraphs for music in motion. Receiptify for a familiar keepsake. The Pudding for exploring time. Dear Data for finding stories in everyday patterns.
